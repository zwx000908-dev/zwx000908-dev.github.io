About me

I am WenXuan Zou, a PhD student at Ocean University of China (OUC). My published work is in organic synthesis and catalysis, including sulfoxonium ylide chemistry and strain-release-driven annulation.

I publish under the name Wen-Xuan Zou.

Research

  • Organic synthesis and selective functionalization
  • Machine learning at the interface of organic chemistry
  • Asymmetric catalysis
  • Transition-metal catalysis and annulation reactions
  • Sulfoxonium ylides and strained bicyclo[1.1.0]butanes

Strain-Release-Driven [4 + 1] Annulation: Leveraging Bicyclo[1.1.0]butanes as C1 Synthons via Rh(III) Carbenoid↗

Shang-Shi Zhang, Wen-Xuan Zou, Zi-Xuan Wan, Can Yu, Jia-Lin Song, Xiang Liu, Cheng Zeng, Xiaohui Cao, Pengju Feng.

Strain releaseRh(III) catalysis[4 + 1] annulation
Research overview+

This study uses strained bicyclo[1.1.0]butanes as one-carbon building blocks in Rh(III)-catalyzed annulation. It expands ring-construction strategies through strain release, with mechanistic calculations supporting the involvement of rhodium carbenoid and metallocyclobutane intermediates.

Research overview+

A selective route to phosphonyl and sulfur-functionalized sulfoxonium ylides using hybrid I(III)/S(VI) reagents. Copper-catalyzed P–H insertion and reactions with sulfur reagents provide complementary ways to diversify these useful synthetic intermediates.
